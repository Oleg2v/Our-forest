# map: home section 5 "Forest map — the checkout" + the forest-map component + demo data

## Files
- `partials/home/map.html`: `section#map.mapsec[data-bg=sand]`. It holds the heading and subtitle, the map mount (`[data-forest-map]`) and the four participation cards. The cards have static prices so they work without JS.
- `src/sections/home/map.ts` (+ `map.css`): mounts the map in idle time, or at once on `#map`. It also syncs the card prices from `prices.ts`, draws the card icons on reveal, handles the deep link and the dev picker.
- `src/components/forest-map.ts` (+ `forest-map.css`): the component. The exported contract is unchanged (`createForestMap`, `Spot`, `SpeciesId`, `ForestMapHandle`). Additions are all optional:
  - `Spot.zone`, `Spot.row`, `Spot.rot`
  - options `kinds`, `intro` and `fullscreen`
  - `export const forestSpots`
- `src/components/forest-map/`:
  - `plan.ts`: plot geometry (pure data)
  - `art.ts`: hand-drawn SVG plan
  - `spots.ts`: markers
  - `camera.ts`: own pan/zoom on GSAP
  - `data.ts`: spots, labels, card HTML, micro-copy
  - `icons.ts`: species and UI line icons
  - `sketch.ts`: seeded RNG, Catmull-Rom curves, wobble, crowns, hulls
- `src/data/trees.json`: 170 spots.
  - 158 trees: 100 planted, 20 reserved, 38 free.
  - Furniture: 2 free benches, 2 free tables, 2 free fire nooks, and 2 built benches with dedications.
  - 4 free corners.
  - It is generated by `scratchpad/agents/map/gen-trees.ts` (`node --experimental-strip-types gen-trees.ts /home/user/Our-forest`). The generator is deterministic and imports `plan.ts`, so trees never stand on paths. Tree ids are zone + sequence. `0427` is a fig planted 14.2.2026, "לכבוד הולדת נועה", "משפחת כהן", public. Planting dates fall in winter seasons, with community days on Tu BiShvat. Reserved trees have planned dates (most on 23.1.2027).

## How it works
- **The plan**: a top view on cream paper, about 1 unit = 10 cm.
  - Inside the fence: walkways from the gate, the fire clearing (stones, flame, log seats), the kitchen under a pergola with an oven and herb beds, the fenced dogs' yard (house, bowl, tunnel, slalom poles, paw prints), the outdoor classroom (bench arcs) and the contour rows (swales). The swales are drawn from the tree data, so they always match.
  - Outside the fence: fields, neighbours' orchards, the wadi, the moshav road and houses with the label "מושב פדיה". You see these when you zoom out.
  - Also a compass, a scale bar and a title cartouche.
  - Ink is non-scaling bark hairlines. Washes are sage or sand. `--fruit` is used only on free places and prices.
- **Markers**:
  - Planted: an olive crown. Each species has its own lobe shape and tone; size grows with age; almonds have blossoms.
  - Reserved: a dashed outline with a stake.
  - Free: a `--fruit` ring.
  - Benches, tables, fire nooks and corners: plan symbols, in fruit when free.
  - Markers scale with about √zoom, so they stay readable.
- **Motion idea**: the plan draws itself when first seen (DrawSVG on the ink, walkways laid). Then the trees are planted in a wave that starts at the gate (`expo.out`). The free places come last with a light elastic pop, and their soft pulse starts. The intro finishes the moment you interact.
  - The pulse is a composited HTML layer under the SVG (transform/opacity only), placed by the camera. A CDP trace shows the idle map does not repaint, and a still cluster view also does no GPU work.
- **No scroll hijack**: wheel and touch scroll the page until the map is clicked or tapped. A hint shows on hover, on wheel over the inactive map, and always on touch.
  - Once active: drag with inertia, wheel or trackpad-pinch zoom anchored at the pointer, double-click to zoom, two-finger pinch, + / − / "כל היער" buttons, and the keys + − 0.
  - Esc, a click outside, the "סיום" button or scrolling the map away releases it.
- **Clusters**: below a zoom threshold (desktop 0.42, touch 0.6, desktop picker 0.3) the trees gather. They slide toward their grove centre and shrink, a canopy blob fades in, and a badge shows the tree count plus a fruit badge with the free count. Clicking a badge zooms to that grove.
- **Phones**: the map fills the screen in the section, and the plan rotates 90° on portrait viewports so the plot fills the screen; labels stay upright.
  - The first tap opens a real full screen (fixed, `lockScroll`, `modal:open`, a clip-path grows from the section rect, focus trap).
  - Tapping a tree opens a bottom sheet (close it with ✕, a swipe down or a tap on the paper). Free places are bigger, with 44px+ hit areas.
- **Desktop hover card**:
  - Planted: species, number, dedication (or "הקדשה פרטית"), who planted, date and occasion. 0427 also has "צפו בעמוד העץ".
  - Free: "המקום הזה מחכה לעץ שלכם", 540 ₪ and "בוחרים את המקום".
  - Clicking a planted tree pins its card; clicking a free place goes straight to the configurator with the leaf transition.
  - Targets:
    - trees → `/plant?spot=ID`, plus `&species=` when a species filter is on
    - benches, tables and fire nooks → `/plant?product=bench&item=bench|table|fire&spot=ID`
    - corners → `/plant?product=corner&spot=ID`
- **Cursor**: trees get `data-cursor-icon` (their species icon), set lazily on hover. Free places get "שתלו" on fruit. The active map gets "גררו".
- **Filters**: species chips and an occasion select. Non-matching trees dim, cluster counts and the list update, and an aria-live line announces the result.
- **Accessibility**:
  - One Tab stop enters the free places (roving tabindex). The arrow keys move spatially and the camera follows. Enter or Space goes to the configurator.
  - The card's button is the next Tab stop. Esc closes the card and returns focus to the place.
  - The "רשימה" toggle shows a full text list. It is the screen-reader list and is always in the DOM (visually hidden while the map is shown).
- **Picker** (`mode:'picker'`): only free places of `kinds` are radios in a radiogroup. `kinds` defaults to the `?product=` in the URL: bench → bench/table/fire, corner → corner, otherwise tree.
  - `onSelect(spot)` fires only on user choice. `select(id)` from the host does not call it.
  - `initialSpotId` is selected and framed. A `select()` that comes before the first layout is deferred.
  - It works in place on phones and never goes full screen.
  - Verified inside the real /plant step "איפה הוא יגדל?": selection syncs both ways with the configurator.
- **Reduced motion**: no intro, no pulse, instant zoom and clustering, and no clip-path animation.
- **Deep link**: `/?spot=0427#map` centres that place and pins its card.
- **Dev**: `/?mappicker=1#map` (optionally `&product=bench|corner&spot=ID`) mounts a picker under the map in dev builds only.

## Known gaps / honest notes
- **The machine was heavily loaded** (load about 7 on 4 cores, other agents' headless browsers), and the software GL made frame rates and timings meaningless. Some screenshots timed out during transitions, and I could not capture the intro mid-way; I only saw its end state. A trace showed the idle and clustered map do no repaint work. Someone should check the intro and the full-screen clip animation on a real phone, as I could not judge their smoothness here.
- The intro animates about 170 tree groups plus DrawSVG in one SVG layer (about 3 s of SVG repaints). On a weak Android this may drop frames; the fallback would be to shorten it.
- Clicking a free place on desktop navigates at once (the card shows on hover first). Some people may expect a click to only show the card.
- Hover on free places plays the chrome's default hover sound (they are `role=button`).
- The legend counts trees only ("כבר ניטע 100"), but the list's planted group also includes the 2 built benches (102).
- Micro-copy not in the brief, kept minimal: the hints, "סיום", "רשימה"/"מפה", "הכל", "כל היער", "הקדשה פרטית", "מקום מס׳", the map labels (מדורה, מטבח, חצר הכלבים, כיתת חוץ, כניסה, צ, 10 מ׳) and the furniture names from the brief's card text. All of it is in `forest-map/data.ts` (`MC`) and `plan.ts`.
- The `data-he` attributes in my partial follow the roots partial. Nothing reads them yet.

## Requests to other owners
- **/plant (steps-choose.ts)**: please pass `kinds` explicitly when the product is bench or corner (`createForestMap(el, { mode:'picker', kinds: ['bench','table','fire'] | ['corner'], ... })`). It already defaults from `?product=`, but an explicit value is safer when the product switches in place: destroy and recreate the map, or pass `kinds`.
- **Wall section**: the dedications from the map are available as `forestSpots` from `components/forest-map`. For a lighter import, use `SPOTS` from `components/forest-map/data`. Filter on `status==='planted' && public`.
- **Tree page / anywhere**: `/?spot=0427#map` opens that tree on the home map.
- **Chrome**: nothing is needed. I use `lockScroll`/`unlockScroll`, `modal:open|close` (ref-counted), `navigate()`, `data-cursor*` and `--mbar-space`. The phone bar's `--mbar-space` lifts the map legend above the bar.
